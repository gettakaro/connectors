Source-only, HEAD 3859fd8. Paths are relative to the repo root.

Issue claim: the shared native runner "passes stack references into work that can continue after timeout".

## Remaining risks (source-only)

| # | Copy | Risk | Severity | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | dune | `RunJson` (`:319-330`) and `[&]` `Run` callers (`http.cpp:260`, `events.cpp:2021`) cause use-after-scope when a started job outlives a 5 s timeout. That is likely during the measured ~18 s ProcessEvent stalls (`:37-42`). | high | Port the dragonwilds `shared_ptr` `RunJson` and convert both `[&]` sites. Port `game_thread_timeout_test.cpp` and its ASan run (`tests/run-timeout.sh:13`). |
| 2 | conan | Uncaught exception in `Drain` (`:85`) wedges the caller forever (`:57`) and unwinds through the engine's ProcessEvent. Unbounded queue (`:47`). No Windows `Stop()`. | med | Wrap `job->fn()` in try/catch and always mark `Done`, add a cap (256 like the siblings), call `Stop()` from Windows teardown, add a unit test for queued-cancel, running-wait and throw. |
| 3 | dragonwilds/vein | Memory-safe, but a timed-out started **mutation** (give/teleport/kick) still applies after the caller reported 503 (`gamethread.cpp:273-277`). That allows a duplicate give on Takaro retry. Cancelled jobs use per-tick slots (`:83`). | med | Report "outcome unknown" (distinct from 503 not-run) for started-then-timed-out jobs, or make mutations idempotent by request id. Pop cancelled jobs without counting them against `kJobsPerTick`. |

Tests that exercise this today: `games/dragonwilds/mod/tests/game_thread_timeout_test.cpp` and the identical vein copy. They cover queued-cancel (`:28-31`), the started job outliving the caller with an owned payload (`:33-51`), `RunJson` after the caller frame dies (`:53-71`) and the 256 cap (`:73-79`). They run under ASan with `detect_stack_use_after_return` (`tests/run-timeout.sh:13`) from `build.sh:79` (dragonwilds) and `build.sh:95` (vein), and in CI through `build.sh --tests` (`.github/workflows/dragonwilds.yml:68`, `vein.yml:62`). Gaps: Dune has no runner test (`games/dune/mod/tests/` has only `unit_test.cpp`), and Conan has no direct `GameThread::Run` test (`l2b_test.cpp:807-812` only drives `Drain` in a loop).
